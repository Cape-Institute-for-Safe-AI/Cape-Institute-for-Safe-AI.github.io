* d/acc definition

D/acc stands for accelerating the following:

  • Differential progress: develop technologies that help society manage complexity, handle risk, and distribute benefits, rather than technologies that just scale raw capability.
  • Defensive advantage: prioritizing tools that make it easier to defend open systems than to attack or control them.
  • Decentralized systems: building infrastructure without central chokepoints, so local actors can still function even when global consensus fails.
  • Democratic participation: giving more people agency in shaping and benefiting from progress, rather than concentrating power in the hands of a few.
