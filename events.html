A thriving community of curious, compassionate, and courageous stewards of the future.

We regularly host events that create community cohesion, raise awareness of AI safety, and engage national AI stakeholders. We host weekly reading groups and meetups at our co-working space in Cape Town. This is a core pillar of our community. Outside of these regular events, we often run workshops at national AI conferences and host talks featuring world-class AI safety researchers. Upcoming events are listed on our Luma calendar.
